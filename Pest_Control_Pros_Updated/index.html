Common Pest Problems We Solve in Corona

Corona sits in a dry inland valley, and that shapes the pest pressure. Ants come in for water, rodents come in when the fields dry out, and roaches thrive in the warm months. Here are the problems we get called for most.

01

Ants marching through the kitchen every morning

We trace the trail to the colony, apply bait gel the ants carry back to the nest, and seal the entry points along the foundation. Most ant infestations in Corona clear up within 7 to 10 days after treatment.

02

Roaches scattering when you turn on the light

Cockroach infestations need a two-visit approach. We apply gel bait and an insect growth regulator on the first visit, then return in 14 days to catch newly hatched roaches. We also treat wall voids and under appliances.

03

Scratching sounds in the attic at night

That is usually rats or mice. We inspect the attic and crawlspace with a UV flashlight, set rodent traps, and seal entry points with exclusion mesh. We also remove nesting material and treat for fleas and mites they carry.

04

Black widow spiders in the garage or woodpile

We treat webs, cracks, and corners with a residual insecticide and remove egg sacs by hand. We also clear the areas around the garage and storage that attract spiders. Most customers see a sharp drop in activity within a week.
